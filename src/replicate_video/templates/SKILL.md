The full style is in [references/style.md](references/style.md) and its measured numbers in
[references/measurements.json](references/measurements.json). Read the style file before writing anything.

## Make a video in this style

1. **Brief.** Get what the video is about and the content that fills the slots in "Make it yours" in the style
   file: copy, names, products, colours, logo, footage. Ask only for what you cannot do without.
2. **Beat map.** Write the beats before any code: for each, its length (use the style's shot lengths and cut
   rate), what fills the frame, the exact on-screen words, the camera move and how it exits.
3. **Build.** {engine_line}
4. **Check against the style, not a reference.** Render, then look at frames at every cut and in the middle of
   every shot. Compare with the numbers: shot lengths, cut rate, camera moves and eases, caption position and
   timing, palette shares, loudness. Fix the largest real difference once, render again, then stop.
5. **Say what you changed** from the style and why.

## Rules

- The style is technique: rhythm, framing, motion, type treatment, palette roles. It does not include the
  references' footage, characters, logos, music, voice or words. Make your own.
- Fonts: use a redistributable family that matches the measured traits; say which one you substituted.
- When the user's brand has its own colours and type, they fill the style's colour and type roles; the rhythm and
  motion stay.
