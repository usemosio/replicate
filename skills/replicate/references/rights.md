# What may be copied

Replicate videos the person has the right to use: their own, a client's with permission, or a reference studied for
technique.

Technique is free to learn: pacing, shot lengths, framing, camera moves, transitions, type treatment, palette
roles, how a joke or a reveal lands. These come from measuring and looking.

Material belongs to its owner, and stays out of the new video unless the person says it is theirs or they hold the
rights:

- footage, frames, stills and screen recordings from the reference;
- characters, mascots, logos and illustrations as drawn in the reference;
- music, voices and sound effects;
- the reference's words, when the new video is for someone else's brand.

When it is theirs, a still (a product shot, a logo) may be cut from the cleanest frame. Anything that performs (a
blink, a wordmark arriving letter by letter) is rebuilt. The reference video itself, and whole reference frames as a
backdrop, never go into the film.

Downloading from a platform is subject to its terms. Keep downloaded references for analysis, out of the project and
out of anything you share.
